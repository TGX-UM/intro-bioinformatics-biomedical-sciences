# Changelog

## 0.2.1 (October 2026)

Corrections to the background chapters of Module 1:

- *Interpreting BLAST output*: the max/total score example said 15 and 25; with one point per
  identical nucleotide the two sub hits give a max score of 30 and a total of 50. Also corrected
  the statement on when max and total are equal: that depends on the number of sub hits, not on
  gaps ([#1](https://github.com/TGX-UM/intro-bioinformatics-biomedical-sciences/issues/1)).
- *Alignment scoring example (for nucleotides)*: replaced a reference to "Case 10" from an
  older course ([#2](https://github.com/TGX-UM/intro-bioinformatics-biomedical-sciences/issues/2)).
- *Introduction*: updated the dated SARS-CoV-2 sentence, added the missing Nextstrain link and
  fixed the Wikipedia codon table link ([#3](https://github.com/TGX-UM/intro-bioinformatics-biomedical-sciences/issues/3)).
- Typos in several chapters ([#6](https://github.com/TGX-UM/intro-bioinformatics-biomedical-sciences/issues/6)).
