# Practical 2: Variants and proteins

*Total time: about 2½ hours.*

APC is a tumour-suppressor gene: inherited variants that disable it cause familial adenomatous
polyposis, and it is mutated in most colorectal cancers. In this practical you place the APC gene
in Ensembl, study its variants in the variant databases dbSNP and ClinVar, and explore the APC
protein in UniProt. Then you compare how dbSNP and Ensembl describe one and the same variant.
