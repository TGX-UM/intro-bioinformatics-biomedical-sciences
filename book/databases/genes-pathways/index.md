# Practical 1: Genes, pathways and compounds

*Total time: about 3 hours.*

Growth is regulated by many molecular interactions, and Insulin-like Growth Factor 1 (IGF-1)
plays a central role in development, growth and muscle and bone biology. In this practical you
follow IGF-1 through a disease database (OMIM), a pathway database (WikiPathways) and a genomic
database (Ensembl). Then you place it in a second pathway, look up compounds in two chemical
databases (ChEBI and PubChem), and study a growth-related gene of your choice in NCBI Gene.
