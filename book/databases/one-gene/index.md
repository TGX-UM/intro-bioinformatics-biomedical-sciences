# Practical 3: One gene through all six databases

*Total time: about 80 minutes. Do [Practical 1](../genes-pathways/index.md) and
[Practical 2](../variants-proteins/index.md) first.*

This practical brings everything together. You choose one gene, TP53 (cancer), LMNA (ageing) or
APOE (Alzheimer disease), and follow it through NCBI Gene, Ensembl, OMIM, UniProt, WikiPathways
and dbSNP/ClinVar: its identifiers, a disease-associated variant, its transcripts and isoforms,
expression and protein function, pathways, phenotypes and inheritance. Along the way you see
where the databases agree, where they differ, and why.
