Biomedical Sciences students at Maastricht University meet bioinformatics in a series of computer
practicals spread across several courses: running sequence searches, querying biological
databases, and exploring pathways and cell signalling. This book brings those practicals together
in one place, with a shared structure, so that you can see how they connect and come back to them
later.

Every practical runs in a web browser. You do not need to install software or write code.

## Modules

| Module | Course | Topic | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1. Sequence alignment and BLAST](blast/index.md) | BBS1001 | Pairwise alignment, scoring matrices, BLASTp, BLASTx and BLASTn on four real cases | Available |
| 2. Biological databases | BBS2002 | OMIM, WikiPathways, Ensembl, NCBI Gene, UniProt and dbSNP | In preparation |
| 3. Cell signalling | BBS2042 | Pathways and cell signalling | Planned |

## How to use this book

Each module opens with what you will learn, how long it takes, and any background reading. The
exercises follow, one page per assignment or case. Under each question there is a collapsed
**Solution** block. Try the question first, then open the solution to check your answer.

Online databases change. Every answer was checked against the live resource on the date shown at
the top of the page, and your numbers may differ slightly if the database has been updated since.
If something no longer matches, please [open an issue](https://github.com/TGX-UM/intro-bioinformatics-biomedical-sciences/issues).
