## Linked, not reproduced

- Proteopedia: the BLAST-OER figure of the β-hemoglobin glutamate (a Proteopedia rendering) was
  left out of this book, because Proteopedia content is licensed GNU FDL. The page is linked instead.

- OMIM (omim.org): link only. OMIM's terms do not allow reproduction for redistribution, so the
  book never includes OMIM screenshots.
- NCBI videos on YouTube: linked, not embedded or copied.
- OMIM in Module 2: the exercises ask students to read OMIM pages, but the book contains no OMIM
  screenshots or OMIM text beyond short identifiers and names.
- PubChem compound pages (Module 2): linked, not shown. A compound page combines sections from
  hundreds of depositors, each under its own terms.
- Videos in Module 2 (NCBI, UniProt) and EMBL-EBI Training courses: linked only.
- Hutchins, *Mol Biol Cell* (doi:10.1091/mbc.E13-10-0602), read in the 2B preparation: linked only.
  It is open access but CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, which is not compatible with CC BY 4.0.
- Wikipedia (*RefSeq*, *Hardy–Weinberg principle*): linked only (CC BY-SA 4.0).
